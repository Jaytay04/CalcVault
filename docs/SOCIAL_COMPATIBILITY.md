## Owner-operated device procedure

For each service independently:

1. Confirm the native hostname display matches the expected official origin before entering anything.
2. Load the landing page in its embedded profile.
3. Perform direct sign-in and any two-factor challenge manually. Do not share or record credentials.
4. Check the signed-in home/feed, scrolling, one video with audio, search, profile, and ordinary links.
5. Lock/background the app and confirm private content is covered and audio stops.
6. Force-quit/relaunch and record whether the session persists.
7. Record login method, date, iPhone model, iOS version, SideStore version, observed errors, and whether an explicit Safari fallback is required.

Do not change the user agent or weaken browser protections to bypass a provider restriction. A Google `disallowed_useragent` or similar provider refusal must be recorded as a compatibility result.
